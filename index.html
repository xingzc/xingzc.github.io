About

I am a Senior Principal Research Scientist at CSIRO, where I lead the Software Engineering for AI (SE4AI) research. My research brings together software engineering, responsible AI, and human-computer interaction.

Over more than 20 years, I have worked across research, education and industry to develop methods and tools for understanding software, supporting developers, and building trustworthy AI-enabled systems. My industry collaborations include Google, Rolls-Royce, and WeBank.

My work has received the ACM SIGSOFT Most Influential Paper Award, six ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Awards and two IEEE TCSE Distinguished Paper Awards.
